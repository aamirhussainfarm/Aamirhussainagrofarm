About Our Farm

Aamir Hussain Agro & Livestock Farm is an agriculture and livestock farming business based in Sindh, Pakistan.


Our activities include livestock rearing, cattle farming, goats and sheep farming, agricultural production, animal feed and other farming-related activities.


Our goal is to develop a modern and sustainable farming business using better livestock management and modern agricultural practices.
