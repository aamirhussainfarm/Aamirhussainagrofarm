About Our Farm

Aamir Hussain Agro & Livestock Farm is an agriculture and livestock farming business based in Sindh, Pakistan.


We are involved in livestock farming, cattle rearing, goat and sheep farming, agricultural production, fodder management and other farming-related activities.


Our aim is to build a sustainable and modern farming business by combining practical farming experience with better livestock management and modern agricultural practices.
